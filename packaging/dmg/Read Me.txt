BikePlanner @VERSION@ — installation and first launch
=====================================================

BikePlanner is not distributed through the App Store and is not notarized by Apple: it is
signed "ad hoc", not with an Apple Developer ID. The first time, macOS refuses to open it
with a message such as:
  "BikePlanner can't be opened because Apple cannot check it for malicious software"
  or "Apple could not verify 'BikePlanner' is free of malware…"
This is expected. The steps below are needed only once.


1. Install
----------
Drag BikePlanner onto the Applications folder in this window, then eject the disk image
("BikePlanner @VERSION@" in the Finder sidebar).


2. First launch — macOS 15 (Sequoia) and later
----------------------------------------------
a. Open the Applications folder and double-click BikePlanner.
   macOS shows a warning: click "Done" (do NOT click "Move to Bin").
b. Open System Settings ▸ Privacy & Security, and scroll down to the "Security" section:
   it says that BikePlanner was blocked. Click "Open Anyway" and enter your password.
c. Double-click BikePlanner again, and click "Open Anyway" in the dialog.


2. First launch — macOS 12 (Monterey) to 14 (Sonoma)
----------------------------------------------------
a. Open the Applications folder.
b. Control-click (or right-click) BikePlanner and choose "Open".
c. Click "Open" in the dialog.


3. Alternative, with the Terminal (all versions)
------------------------------------------------
Copy BikePlanner to Applications, then run in Terminal:
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BikePlanner.app
and open BikePlanner normally.


After that, BikePlanner opens normally with a double-click.

On first launch, BikePlanner asks for permission to use your location (only to center the
map). It needs an Internet connection: maps (OpenStreetMap, Thunderforest, Apple), routing
(brouter.de), points of interest (OpenStreetMap / Overpass) and Google Street View.

BikePlanner runs on macOS 12.7 or later, on Intel and Apple Silicon Macs.
https://github.com/banyanshade-software/BikePlanner
